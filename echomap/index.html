EchoMap Recorder

Plays the chirp train and records raw microphone audio as a 48 kHz WAV.

Two phones side by side: start Record only on one, then Play only on the other within 3 seconds. Play + record on one phone sends sound to the earpiece on iOS.
iOS can move sound to the earpiece while the mic is on. If a recording is too quiet, try another method and keep the one where the chirps come loudly from the bottom speaker. Put the true distance in the name, for example wall_0.75m or open_air.
